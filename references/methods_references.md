# Methods references

* Richards S. et al. (2015). *Standards and guidelines for the interpretation of sequence variants* (ACMG/AMP). Genetics in Medicine 17:405-424. Source of the BA1 / BS1 / PM2 population-frequency criteria and the five-tier classification scheme.
* Chen T., Guestrin C. (2016). *XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System.* KDD.
* Cortes C., Vapnik V. (1995). *Support-vector networks.* Machine Learning 20:273-297.
* Platt J. (1999). *Probabilistic outputs for support vector machines.* The source of the SVM probability calibration used here.
* Gorishniy Y. et al. (2021). *Revisiting Deep Learning Models for Tabular Data* (FT-Transformer). NeurIPS.
* Vaswani A. et al. (2017). *Attention Is All You Need.* NeurIPS.
* Bergstra J., Bengio Y. (2012). *Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization.* JMLR 13:281-305.
* Benjamini Y., Hochberg Y. (1995). *Controlling the false discovery rate.* JRSS-B 57:289-300.
* Youden W. J. (1950). *Index for rating diagnostic tests.* Cancer 3:32-35.
* Tukey J. W. (1977). *Exploratory Data Analysis.* The source of the IQR outlier fences.
* In-silico tools: CADD (Rentzsch 2019), REVEL (Ioannidis 2016), SIFT (Ng & Henikoff 2003), PolyPhen-2 (Adzhubei 2010), SpliceAI (Jaganathan 2019), GERP++ (Davydov 2010), PhyloP (Pollard 2010), gnomAD (Karczewski 2020).
